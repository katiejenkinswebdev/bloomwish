How your garden grows

Four movements, repeated gently.

Plant it

Speak or type your wish. A warm coach asks a few deepening questions and writes it into a vivid scene — your dream, already real, in the second person. Tuck a note inside for harvest day.

Step inside

The heart of Bloomwish: a ninety-second ritual that walks you through your own scene, line by line, and asks one question — where do you feel it in your body?

Tend it

Water with breathwork, affirmations, and bedtime drifting. Feed with deeper practices. Log real-world signs — every one becomes a firefly on your plant.

Harvest it

When a wish comes true out in the world, harvest it. It lives on forever — a keepsake in your greenhouse, a gold charm on the wishing tree, a butterfly that never leaves.

The daily return

Ninety seconds inside the feeling

Grown gently, on purpose

The rules this garden is built on.

  • No streaks, ever. Miss a week and your garden greets you warmly — nothing here shames you back.
  • Dreams never die. A neglected plant rests; a two-minute ritual wakes it whenever you're ready.
  • Release with thanks. Outgrow a wish? Thank it, let it return to the soil, and it feeds the rest of your garden.
  • Quiet notifications. Each one carries a real micro-practice — never a guilt trip.
  • Your data is yours. Export everything or delete your account, in one tap, anytime.

Bloomwish is a mindfulness and visualization practice. It isn't medical or mental-health care, and no outcome is ever promised — what we promise is a beautiful place to return to the feeling of your dreams, daily.

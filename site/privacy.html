Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Bloomwish is a visualization and mindfulness app. Your dreams are personal, so this policy is written to be read: what we collect, why, and the controls you have. We never sell your data, never show ads, and never use your content for advertising or to train AI models.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and an optional display name if you choose to share one.
  • Your garden: the dreams, scenes, capsule notes, signs, reflections, and optional photos you create in the app.
  • Purchases: your subscription status, processed by RevenueCat and the app stores. We never see your payment details.
  • Usage: which screens you visit and core actions you take (via PostHog), and crash reports (via Sentry), tied to your app user ID — used only to fix problems and improve the app.

What we don't collect

  • Voice recordings: dictation is processed on your device. Recordings never leave your phone — only text you choose to keep is stored.
  • Location history: if you turn on the optional weather mirror, your approximate location is used on your device to fetch local weather conditions and is not stored by us. It's off by default.
  • Tracking: no advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, no device fingerprinting, no sale or sharing of personal data for advertising.

How your content is used

To power the app's features, your dream text and scenes are sent from our servers to AI providers — Anthropic (coaching text: questions, scenes, nudges, meditation scripts) and ElevenLabs (meditation audio) — solely to generate your content, and for no other purpose. Generated meditations are stored so you can replay them.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored with Supabase on managed cloud infrastructure, encrypted in transit, with row-level security restricting every record to its owner. Access from the app requires your authenticated session.

Your controls

  • Export: download everything you've created anytime from the Profile screen.
  • Delete: permanently delete your account and all of its data anytime from the Profile screen — no email required, no retention.
  • Notifications: every reminder type can be turned off individually in Preferences.
  • Questions or requests: email support@bloomwish.app and we'll respond within a few days.

Children

Bloomwish is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll note it here with a new date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

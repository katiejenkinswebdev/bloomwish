What we collect

Your account email; an optional display name; the dreams, scenes, signs, reflections, and photos you create; purchase status (via RevenueCat); and product-usage analytics (via PostHog). Dictation audio is processed on your device — recordings never leave your phone; only transcripts you choose to keep are stored.

How we use it

To run the app: your dream text is sent to our AI providers (Anthropic for coaching text, ElevenLabs for meditation audio) solely to generate your content. We never sell data, never show ads, and never use your content for advertising.

How do I cancel Full Bloom?

Subscriptions are managed by your device, not by us — so cancelling really is two taps: your phone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions (iPhone) or the Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions (Android). Your access continues to the end of the paid period. There are no cancellation hoops on our side, ever.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Full Bloom screen in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same store account you bought with.

How do I export or delete my data?

Both live in the app: Profile → Export my data sends you everything you've created; Profile → Delete account permanently removes your account and all of its data. Deletion is immediate and complete — we keep nothing.

My plant is wilting — did I lose progress?

No. Plants rest, they never die. A wilting or dormant dream keeps everything you've grown, and a revive ritual brings the color back whenever you're ready.

The meditation won't generate

Composition takes a minute or two and needs a connection. If it fails, the app will say so — try again in a little while, and email us if it keeps happening. Generated meditations are saved, so replays work offline.
